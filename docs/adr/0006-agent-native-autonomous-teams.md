# Make autonomous agent teams the primary operators

Beacon is primarily a workplace for Agents, observed and bounded by one human steward. Only the
Operator may admit, suspend, or retire persistent Resident Agents. A fresh Installation proposes an
editable Starter Organization with Lead, Builder, Reviewer, and Researcher role templates. It
creates no Resident or authority until the Operator reviews the roster, resource bindings,
permissions, and budgets. Optional Display Names never carry identity or authority. Residents may
create Missions aligned with an Operator-authored Project Charter, form Teams, assign work, and
create Specialist Agents without waiting for routine operator dispatch when Project Policy has
already delegated the necessary capability, time, network, and budget bounds.

## Consequences

The human interface centers on Briefings, exceptions, intervention, and deliberate contribution.
Onboarding avoids both an expert-only blank canvas and an active canned workforce the Operator did
not choose. The Starter Organization can be accepted, edited, or discarded.
Policy chooses a Team Lead using capability fit, availability, and an evidence-linked Track Record
rather than a composite score; the Operator can override that choice. Check-ins are triggered by
risk, spend, failure, drift, or an operator request rather than elapsed time alone. Agents cannot
expand their own Charter, Policy, credentials, capabilities, network reach, or budget. Specialists
receive narrow Mission-scoped identity and end with their Assignment, while their Artifacts,
events, and Receipts remain attributable.
