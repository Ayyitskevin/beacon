# Keep the complete local and self-hosted product free

Beacon's downloadable product and protocol-compatible self-hosted Cloud Link remain complete and
free under Apache-2.0, without a time limit. The optional official hosted Cloud Link costs $5 per
month and funds managed sync, storage, and service operation rather than withholding product
capability.

## Consequences

Core authority, auditability, recovery, agent operation, extension support, preference sync, and
preference version history cannot be degraded to manufacture a paid tier. A user may stay local or
self-host indefinitely. Moving to official hosting must be a simple, optional in-app choice and
does not grant remote access or execution authority. Disconnecting it does not stop local use.

The hosted subscription pays for Beacon to operate the sync infrastructure. It does not unlock a
capability unavailable to self-hosters. Hosted device, storage, and retention limits remain a
separate product decision.
