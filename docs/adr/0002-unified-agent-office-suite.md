# Build one project-centered office suite for agent teams

Beacon is one desktop product where autonomous AI agent teams work under one human steward.
Resident Agents form Teams, create Missions, assign work, and create bounded Specialists while the
operator supplies outcomes, policy, budget, and exceptional authority. Athena, Buzz, Minerva,
Vulcan, ReplayDeck, Janus, and Icarus become capability modules in one codebase and one store, not
live products federated behind a shell. Projects are the long-lived center, Missions are
finite outcomes, and the suite apps are lenses over shared objects. This deliberately replaces the
earlier read-only federating desk because that design preserved the fragmentation Beacon exists to
remove.

The domain is all agent work. Software production is the first vertical slice because its effects,
checks, rollback, and receipts force the general platform to become trustworthy.

## Consequences

The operator interface emphasizes Briefings and exceptions rather than dispatching routine work.
The old Python adapters survive only as migration-reader research. Legacy systems retire on their
own schedules and are never deleted merely because Beacon implements their capability. Imported
and native records may coexist indefinitely, so provenance is permanent product state.

Beacon proves one-Operator Workspaces first. Multi-Owner Workspaces are a later product phase after
the solo product is stable and its authority boundaries pass sustained use. Owner is the only human
role; every Owner uses an individual Operator Account. Shared ownership must preserve individual
identity, attribution, least privilege, conflict handling, and complete audit history rather than
introducing shared credentials or ambient authority. Every Owner may invite or remove another Owner
through their own attributable identity.

The production-application sequence is Software, then Research, then Operations. Operations reuses
the same Projects, Missions, Attention Items, Proposals, and Receipts rather than creating a
separate operations control plane.
