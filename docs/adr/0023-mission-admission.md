# Admit Missions before selecting leadership or granting execution authority

A Mission is a finite, Project-scoped outcome owned by one Team. Its authenticated Operator
admission binds the exact current Charter Revision and every Team member's exact current Resident
Configuration inside one immediate transaction, preserving the desired outcome, success
conditions, constraints, prohibitions, `admitted` lifecycle, actor, and time as immutable evidence.

Beacon does not select a Team Lead in this path. Mission-specific leadership requires a Policy
decision informed by capability fit, availability, and attributable Track Record, none of which
has a complete decision path yet. Inventing a lead during admission would turn Team order or role
labels into ambient authority. Resident-created Missions likewise wait for authenticated Agent
commands and Policy adjudication.

## Consequences

The admitted Mission and its ordered member-configuration snapshot are immutable. Later Charter,
configuration, lifecycle, or activation changes do not rewrite that evidence. Future Mission
lifecycle changes must be append-only decisions rather than updates to the admission record.

Every accepted admission command creates a distinct Mission identity, even when its brief and Team
match an existing Mission. Admission creates no Team Lead, Assignment, Route, Invocation, Run,
credential, budget, network scope, external effect, or execution authority.

This proposal becomes accepted only with Product Steward sign-off on the reviewed branch.
