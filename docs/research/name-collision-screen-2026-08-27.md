# Mosaic final-name collision screen

Date: 2026-08-27

## Scope and method

This is preliminary naming triage, not legal clearance. The screen tested exactly ten uncommon real
words against the product brief: a durable knowledge institution, professional with a quiet academic
edge, and reasonably easy to pronounce and spell after hearing once.

Search started with the fleet's local SearXNG instance, then used current web search and first-party
product, GitHub, package-registry, app-store, dictionary, and registry sources. Exact `.com`
registration was checked through Verisign's authoritative RDAP service. RDAP proves only current
registration data; it does not establish trademark rights, availability in other TLDs, or
willingness to sell.

No candidate received a comprehensive trademark search. The USPTO advises searching similar marks,
coordinated classes, and likelihood of confusion, while WIPO warns that its global database does not
replace national and regional registers. This screen did not assess common-law use, corporate names,
phonetic equivalents, logos, international classes, or jurisdiction-specific risk.
**Absence of search results is not clearance.** A trademark professional should clear any finalist
before public use, registration, or launch.

Trademark references: [USPTO Trademark Search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/search) ·
[WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) ·
[ICANN on RDAP](https://www.icann.org/rdap/)

## Survivor ranking

“Survivor” means only that this pass did not find a disqualifying direct collision. It does not
mean available or legally clear.

1. **Colonnade — Advance.** Best balance of pronunciation, institutional architecture, calm tone,
   and low direct AI-workspace collision in this pass. The exact `.com` is unavailable and broader
   commercial use is substantial.
2. **Chapterhouse — Hold.** Memorable and institution-like, but its religious history and existing
   technology and education uses make it a reserve rather than a launch recommendation.

No defensible third survivor emerged. **Exedra** initially looked viable, but a follow-up found
several exact current software platforms. If Colonnade does not clear professional review and
audience testing, run a second round instead of relaxing the collision standard.

## Candidate findings

### 1. Colonnade

- **Meaning and fit:** A regularly spaced row of columns, usually supporting a roof. It suggests
  a durable institutional structure whose distinct pillars form one coherent whole—an apt metaphor
  for an app suite assembled from several capabilities.
- **Pronunciation:** `KAH-luh-nayd`. Familiar sounds and a largely hear-once spelling.
- **Current collision screen:** No obvious exact-name AI-agent office suite or developer workspace
  surfaced in the local and current web searches. The word is used by established insurance,
  property, hospitality, and financial businesses, so the commercial namespace is not empty.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `colonnade.com` is registered, created 1995-04-21, registrar Epik LLC.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** No comprehensive exact/similar-mark or class 9/42/41 search was
  performed; extensive non-software commercial use could still create conflicts.
- **Verdict:** **Advance** to professional trademark review and audience testing.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/colonnade) ·
  [GitHub repository search](https://github.com/search?q=colonnade&type=repositories) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/COLONNADE.COM)
- **Confidence:** Medium — the direct software signal is encouraging, but broad commercial use and
  an occupied `.com` leave substantial untested risk.

### 2. Exedra

- **Meaning and fit:** An ancient Greek or Roman room or columned recess furnished with seats for
  conversation. It evokes a permanent place where a learned body works and deliberates.
- **Pronunciation:** `EK-suh-druh`; some dictionaries also recognize `ek-SEE-druh`. The variant is a
  usability weakness despite the short spelling.
- **Current collision screen:** An exact `exedra` package exists on PyPI. Exedra Gate is a current
  private-capital platform, ExedraOS is an enterprise application platform, and Schréder EXEDRA is
  a cloud-based smart-city management system. These exact software uses materially crowd the name.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `exedra.com` is registered, created 1996-11-19, registrar Tucows Domains
  Inc.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** Package, company, common-law, phonetic, and international uses
  were not exhaustively searched by class or jurisdiction.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/exedra) ·
  [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/exedra/) · [Exedra Gate](https://www.exedragate.com/) ·
  [ExedraOS](https://www.exedraos.com/) ·
  [Schréder EXEDRA](https://www.schreder.com/en/schreder-exedra-platform-smart-lighting-cms) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/EXEDRA.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — multiple exact current software platforms plus pronunciation variants
  conflict with both the collision and hear-once requirements.

### 3. Chapterhouse

- **Meaning and fit:** A permanent room or building where a cathedral, monastery, or collegiate
  chapter meets and conducts its affairs. It naturally conveys an institution, its members, its
  records, and a place of deliberation.
- **Pronunciation:** `CHAP-ter-house`. Easy to hear, say, and spell.
- **Current collision screen:** `chapterhouse.co` is an established SaaS-product organization, and
  OverSkill offers an AI-customizable Chapterhouse education-management app template. These are
  adjacent technology uses even though neither is an AI-agent office suite.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `chapterhouse.com` is registered, created 1997-05-02, registrar
  MarkMonitor Inc.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** Exact and similar marks, the technology-services overlap, and the
  term's religious and organizational uses require manual class and common-law review.
- **Verdict:** **Hold** as a reserve, not a launch recommendation.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/chapter%20house) ·
  [Chapterhouse](https://chapterhouse.co/) ·
  [OverSkill template](https://www.overskill.com/templates/chapterhouse) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/CHAPTERHOUSE.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — the meaning is strong and the adjacent commercial uses are directly
  observable; legal significance remains unknown.

### 4. Collegium

- **Meaning and fit:** An organization of peers with similar work or interests, especially in a
  university. It maps well to a round table of agents without naming a single Agent or operator.
- **Pronunciation:** `kuh-LEE-jee-um`. Pronounceable, though the final spelling may need one prompt.
- **Current collision screen:** Collegium is already the name of a live Canvas AI Study Assistant
  with a Chrome extension and `collegiumai.app`. This is a direct AI-product collision in an
  adjacent academic workspace.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `collegium.com` is registered, created 1998-09-19, registrar NameCheap,
  Inc.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** The existing AI product makes a full confusion and class review
  necessary; this screen did not determine registration or territorial scope.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:**
  [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/collegium) ·
  [Collegium product](https://collegiumai.app/about) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/COLLEGIUM.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — the exact AI-study product is current, direct, and close in category.

### 5. Carrel

- **Meaning and fit:** An enclosed or partitioned library desk for individual study. It captures the
  calm workstation idea, although it emphasizes solitary work more than an institutional suite.
- **Pronunciation:** `CARE-uhl`; dictionaries also record variants, weakening hear-once certainty.
- **Current collision screen:** Current exact-name Carrel projects provide an academic research
  environment, agent skill pack, Python CLI, Rust terminal app, and published Rust crates. That is a
  direct collision with the proposed product's research and agent-tooling surface.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `carrel.com` is registered, created 1997-06-24, registrar Tucows Domains
  Inc.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** No determination was made about ownership links among the current
  projects, registrations, app stores, or common-law geographic scope.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** despite the excellent metaphor.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/carrel) ·
  [Carrel repository](https://github.com/linxule/carrel) ·
  [carrel crate](https://docs.rs/crate/carrel/latest) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/CARREL.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — the exact active research-tooling collision is unusually close.

### 6. Rotunda

- **Pronunciation:** `roh-TUN-duh`. Easy to pronounce and spell.
- **Current collision screen:** Rotunda is an active agent-first browser and installable agent
  skill. It is directly aimed at agents and software automation.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `rotunda.com` is registered, created 1993-07-08, registrar DNC Holdings,
  Inc.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** The current agent-browser product is enough for product triage;
  registrations, classes, and geographic scope were not legally analyzed.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/rotunda) ·
  [Rotunda repository](https://github.com/MonkeySee-AI/rotunda) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/ROTUNDA.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — this is an exact, current, directly agent-oriented software collision.

- **Trademark-search limitation:** This screen did not resolve which uses have registrations, but

- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/scriptorium) ·

- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `athenaeum.com` is registered, created 2000-11-20, registrar Gandi SAS.

- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.

### 10. Studium

- **Meaning and fit:** A medieval term for a place or center of instruction, sometimes the customary
  name for a university. It is short, sober, and one of the strongest literal institutional fits.
- **Pronunciation:** `STOO-dee-um`. Easy to say; listeners may initially spell it “studio.”
- **Current collision screen:** Active exact-name products include an AI-assisted study platform at
  `studium.fyi`, a course-sharing platform at `studium.gent`, a classroom communication repository,
  and multiple AI study-companion products. The category is crowded even though it is education-
  rather than agent-office-focused.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `studium.com` is registered, created 1996-07-11, registrar GoDaddy.com,
  LLC.
- **Trademark-search limitation:** No legal analysis of the active education products' territories,
  classes, or registrations was performed.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [historical definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studium_generale) ·
  [Studium AI study platform](https://studium.fyi/) ·

- **Confidence:** High — several exact active products occupy the same academic-software

## Conclusion

This pass rejects eight of ten candidates because exact current products already occupy adjacent
AI, agent, research, writing, education, workspace, or software-platform categories. **Colonnade**
is the only name to advance. **Chapterhouse** is a reserve. Both exact `.com` domains are already
registered, and neither has received trademark clearance.
