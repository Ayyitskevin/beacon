# Name-screen addendum: Beacon, Recall, and Hivemind

Date: 2026-08-27

## Scope and method

This is preliminary naming triage, not legal clearance. The screen tests exactly three names against
the product brief: an installable, open-source, local-first office suite and workstation for
autonomous AI-agent teams under one human operator. The intended tone is a professional institution
with a quiet academic character, and the name should denote the workplace or suite rather than an
individual Agent.

Research used current first-party product sites, official repositories, Microsoft documentation,
and Verisign's authoritative `.com` RDAP service. RDAP confirms registration records only; it does
not establish trademark rights, acquisition availability, or safety in any product class or
jurisdiction. No comprehensive trademark, company-name, common-law, phonetic, international-class,
or jurisdiction-specific search was performed. **Absence of search results is not clearance.**
No source contradiction affecting the verdicts was found: the products differ in scope, but their
overlapping exact-name uses compound rather than reduce confusion.

## Comparative result

| Candidate | Semantic fit | Direct collision risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beacon | Clear guidance metaphor, but more signal or feature than institution | Multiple exact AI-agent platforms, including a local-first developer tool | **Reject** |
| Recall | Strong memory metaphor, but a verb/feature rather than a workplace | Several exact AI knowledge, desktop, meeting, and context-switching products | **Reject** |
| Hivemind | Immediately conveys a coordinated agent collective, but not its workplace | Exact-name open-source multi-agent office and cross-agent memory products | **Reject** |

None should join or replace **Colonnade** as the provisional leader. Each is easier to say and spell
than Colonnade, but the direct category conflicts outweigh that advantage.

## Candidate findings

### 1. Beacon

- **Meaning and semantic fit:** A beacon guides or signals. The word is professional, familiar,
  easy to pronounce, and easy to spell, but it describes guidance or visibility rather than the
  durable institution where a team works. It would fit a monitoring surface or alerting subsystem
  better than the complete suite.
- **Current direct collisions:** Vexor Labs already publishes **Beacon**, an open-source,
  local-first
  debugging platform for AI agents. Another open-source Beacon collects local Claude Code, Codex,
  Hermes, OpenCode, and Pi activity into a local dashboard and exposes it through MCP. Beacon
  Security calls itself an AI-native security platform, includes an Agent Runtime, and says it was
  built for AI agents to run on. A separate Beacon AI product provides voice and chat agents,
  knowledge bases, workflows, and analytics. These are exact current uses in the agent, platform,
  developer-tool, observability, and operations neighborhoods.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `beacon.com` is registered. Verisign RDAP reports creation on 1992-07-22
  and expiration on 2027-07-21.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Vexor Labs Beacon](https://github.com/vexorlabs/beacon) ·
  [local agent-activity Beacon](https://github.com/johnnygreco/beacon) ·
  [Beacon Security](https://beacon.security/) ·
  [Beacon AI](https://www.beaconjo.com/features) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/BEACON.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — an exact open-source, local-first AI-agent developer platform is a direct
  collision, independently reinforced by other current agent-platform uses.

### 2. Recall

- **Meaning and semantic fit:** Recall evokes institutional memory and resuming interrupted work. It
  is exceptionally easy to say and spell, but it names a cognitive action or feature rather than a
  workplace, institution, or suite. It also creates ambiguity in phrases such as “open Recall”
  and “recall this run.”
- **Current direct collisions:** Recall is already a self-organizing personal AI knowledge base with
  a Notion-style editor and Obsidian-like graph. Microsoft Recall is a Windows 11 feature for
  finding and returning to past PC activity. Recall.ai is a developer platform and conversation-data
  ingestion layer with organization workspaces. JoinRecall offers a downloadable AI context-
  switching agent that restores unfinished work. The official Recall knowledge-base site itself
  warns that users already confuse its product with Microsoft Recall and Recall.ai.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `recall.com` is registered. Verisign RDAP reports creation on 1999-01-11
  and expiration on 2027-01-11.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Recall personal AI knowledge base](https://www.recall.it/about) ·
  [Microsoft Recall](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/ai/apis/recall) ·
  [Recall.ai workspaces](https://docs.recall.ai/docs/environments) ·
  [JoinRecall](https://www.joinrecall.com/) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/RECALL.COM)
- **Confidence:** High — several prominent exact-name products occupy knowledge, desktop,
  developer-platform, workspace, and personal-productivity categories, with first-party evidence of
  existing market confusion.

### 3. Hivemind

- **Meaning and semantic fit:** Hivemind directly communicates many intelligences acting as one. It
  is memorable, pronounceable, and conceptually close to coordinated agent teams, but it names the
  collective intelligence rather than its workplace. Its biological and science-fiction overtones
  also conflict with the requested restrained institutional tone.
- **Current direct collisions:** Hivementality's **Hivemind** is already a self-hosted, open-source
  multi-agent platform. Its documentation explicitly calls Hivemind “the office,” says agents
  live and work there, and includes workspaces, team chat, tools, Codex delegation, and autonomous
  scheduling. Another open-source, local-first Hivemind is a project mission-control canvas for
  Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, OpenCode, and Pi, with workspaces, issues, live diffs, remote SSH, and
  parallel agent execution. Activeloop also ships **Hivemind**, a shared brain and skill-propagation
  layer for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other agents. A fourth exact-name product at
  `hivemind.ai` describes itself as a social AI. The first two collisions are almost identical to
  parts of the proposed category.
- **Domain/RDAP signal:** `hivemind.com` is registered. Verisign RDAP reports creation on 1996-07-23
  and expiration on 2031-07-22.
- **Verdict:** **Reject** from the final-name pool.
- **Sources:** [Hivementality Hivemind](https://github.com/hivementality-ai/hivemind) ·
  [local-first mission-control Hivemind](https://github.com/dip497/hivemind) ·
  [Activeloop Hivemind](https://github.com/activeloopai/hivemind) ·
  [Hivemind social AI](https://www.hivemind.ai/) ·
  [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/HIVEMIND.COM)
- **Confidence:** Very high — the name is already used by an active, installable, open-source
  office for autonomous multi-agent teams and by another cross-agent knowledge product.

## Recommendation

Keep **Colonnade** as the provisional leader and reject Beacon, Recall, and Hivemind from the final
name pool. Of the three, **Beacon** has the best tone and usability, but the exact local-first
AI-agent developer-platform collision makes it unsuitable for the whole product. **Recall** is too
crowded and feature-like. **Hivemind** has the closest literal concept and the most disqualifying
collision.

If the team likes these semantic directions, preserve them as naming prompts rather than names:
seek a word for a **place that guides**, a **place that remembers**, or a **place where many minds
work together**. A second round should retain the collision standard instead of weakening it.
