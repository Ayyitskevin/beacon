## Destination and accepted constraints

The delta is a reviewable contract between attribution, admission, recovery evidence and Attention—not another absence inventory. Existing [AC22:29–35](assignment-recovery-boundary.md#L29), [AC27:28–32](attention-notification-boundary.md#L28) and [AC40:9–11](attention-history-boundary.md#L9) remain the witness baseline.

**Accepted, not provisional:** an Assignment relates accountable Agent and work; Handoff preserves state, context, Artifacts, risks, verification and authorship ([CONTEXT:147–164](../../../CONTEXT.md#L147)). Eligible failure permits at most one automatic Recovery Attempt under unchanged Proposal, authority, Assignment and remaining budget; second failure pauses the Assignment and leads to eligible reassignment/Handoff or Attention when safe reassignment is impossible ([ADR0010:7–15](../../adr/0010-one-recovery-then-handoff.md#L7); [BLUEPRINT:369–373](../../BLUEPRINT.md#L369)).

**Accepted:** Attention requires unresolved condition, named eligible actor and concrete next action; enforced refusals are not automatically Attention. Only Attention may request OS notification ([BLUEPRINT:347–359](../../BLUEPRINT.md#L347)). Budget hierarchy, ancestor roll-up, parent control of unused capacity and the shared-parent overspend prohibition already exist in [BLUEPRINT:432–436,1158–1159](../../BLUEPRINT.md#L432); the memo does not replace them.

## Existing owners and contract boundary

The accepted module map places Assignments/attention in **Work**, Invocations/usage in **Inference**, bindings/Rulings in **Authority**, and Runs/Receipts in **Execution**; storage stays private behind typed calls ([BLUEPRINT:637–649](../../BLUEPRINT.md#L637)). These are responsibility boundaries, not claims that the proposed calls exist. beacond remains sole store writer/effects authority; accepted Commands pair domain changes with audit events ([AGENTS:139–150,212](../../../AGENTS.md#L139)).

Existing `TeamService::form` uses an authenticated store, current configuration checks and Operator attribution; its writer stores the mapped actor and formation event ([team.rs:334–374,421–460](../../../crates/beacond/src/team.rs#L334)). Its returned errors do not themselves demonstrate the **PROVISIONAL M6-A3** denial-record obligation ([memo:11](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L11)). Existing Run reconstruction and installation Resume are not Assignment continuation ([AC22:17–23](assignment-recovery-boundary.md#L17)).

The [source/compatibility map](m6-source-compatibility.md) records that the inspected Team/Mission SQL checks admit only `operator`. **PROVISIONAL M6-A1/M6-A2/M6-A4** do not select a Resident-issued record family or authorize changing those checks; this compatibility question remains parked under Q2.

`InvocationStoreService::get` returns scoped, verified historical evidence—not authority, admission or resend capability ([model_invocation_store.rs:1186–1231](../../../crates/beacond/src/model_invocation_store.rs#L1186)). The codec's retained authority digest is a claim, and retained byte limits are not allocation budgets ([model_invocation.rs:148–176](../../../crates/beacond/src/model_invocation.rs#L148)). `MeasuredSpendService::record` verifies/stores evidence; the record is not a reservation or budget verdict ([spend.rs:6–10,1925–1949](../../../crates/beacond/src/spend.rs#L6)). Replacing these distinctions with a single “ready” result would erase evidence boundaries.

## Conceptual calls: information obligations, not signatures or schema

**T — Attribute a work record.** Conceptual caller information is the acting identity and the work/record being attributed, not authority conferred by a chosen representation. Named-value mapping and explicit coverage constrain the observable recorded actor (**PROVISIONAL M6-A1/M6-A2**); older values must remain readable and round-trip (**PROVISIONAL M6-A4**). A denied request needs separately observable actor + reason evidence (**PROVISIONAL M6-A3**). These are review obligations, not selected variants, persisted names or a new storage owner ([memo:9–12](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L9)). Unknown actor attribution stops this branch at Q2; no substitute identity is supplied.

**R — Consider Assignment recovery.** Caller information: accountable Assignment/Agent/Mission context, observed failure/uncertainty, existing Run/Receipt evidence, prior recovery lineage, and unchanged Proposal/bindings/remaining-budget context. These categories follow the accepted recovery contract; no identifier format or eligibility algorithm is selected. Observable outcomes must distinguish a distinct allowed recovery attempt from second-failure Assignment pause and Handoff, preserving earlier evidence. An unconfirmed Run is not proof of transient retry eligibility ([AC22:21,29–35](assignment-recovery-boundary.md#L21)). The eligibility and lineage-owner branches stop at Q1 below.

**I — Consider an Invocation arising from work/recovery.** Conceptual caller information includes the requesting Resident, Invocation context and the Resident's grant for the explicit Invocation-time check (**PROVISIONAL M6-C1**, [memo:16](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L16)); handle possession is insufficient. Applicable parent/child budget context and usage observations are required to review bounded allocation and metering (**PROVISIONAL M6-C2/M6-C3**, [memo:17–18](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L17)). Required observables are the admission check's disposition and attributable accounting evidence, not authority inferred from a stored Invocation. Whether an Invocation refusal is a command covered by **PROVISIONAL M6-A3** is unresolved under Q2 ([memo:11](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L11)); no command coverage is selected here. Grant scope, accounting mechanics and denial persistence remain Q2–Q3. No sequence between admission, reservations, charging and recording is chosen beyond the specified Invocation-time check.

**A — Read an actionable condition / present its next action.** Conceptual input is the unresolved condition, named actor with eligibility evidence, concrete action and associated work context; caller assertions are not authority. Output must distinguish a genuine current actionable Item from history, read failure or ordinary Activity. Older still-actionable exceptions remain current despite acknowledged-history age ([BLUEPRINT:281–287](../../BLUEPRINT.md#L281); [AC40:9–11](attention-history-boundary.md#L9)). For Resident-issued denial records within the scope ultimately ratified or replaced by Kevin, actor + reason evidence (**PROVISIONAL M6-A3**, [memo:11](m6-provisional-dispositions.md#L11)) does **not** automatically invoke this Attention boundary: the accepted three predicates still need establishment. Presentation grants no action permission ([AGENTS:150](../../../AGENTS.md#L150)). Lifecycle/clearing and eligibility ownership stop at Q4.
