Outcome fidelity — maintainer-authored draft rubric, version 1

This proposed profile is not accepted product Policy and grants no authority.
Evaluate the exact candidate against its frozen owner-verified sources. The candidate alone
is insufficient evidence to apply this rubric. Do not infer that checks ran or work succeeded.

completion_fidelity: Does the document faithfully represent the recorded completion,
its disposition, and each declared success condition without inventing a new success verdict?

evidence_fidelity: Are the document's evidence and delivered-Artifact claims supported
by the exact frozen source records and their cited identities?

honesty_states: Does the document preserve verified, absent, broken and not-evaluated
states without converting missing or inconclusive evidence into a positive or negative judgment?

limitations_and_authority: Does the document retain recorded unfinished work and uncertainty,
state the limits of its evidence, and avoid treating Evaluation or publication as permission?
